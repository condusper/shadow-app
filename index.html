Permissions & calibration

Microphone

Recorded with echo cancellation, noise suppression and auto gain off for an accurate raw voice.

Camera (optional)

Front-camera picture-in-picture to compare mouth movement. Recorded and shown locally only, never uploaded. Off by default.

Latency calibration

Plays a click and records it to measure the round-trip delay between playback and your microphone. Used to make timing feedback more honest.
